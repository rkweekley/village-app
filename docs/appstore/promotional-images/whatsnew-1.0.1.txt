Welcome to Village — our first release.

Village gives your whole family one shared place to run the week:

• Chores — assign chores and let kids earn points as they finish them
• Rewards — create rewards kids can redeem with the points they've earned
• Calendar — a shared family calendar everyone can see and add to
• Meals — plan the week's meals and build a shared shopping list
• Tasks — keep school work and household to-dos in one list
• Family — add everyone in seconds with a simple family code

Village Monthly and Village Annual unlock full access for the whole family. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and can be managed any time in your App Store account settings.
